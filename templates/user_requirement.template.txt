## Intake Rule
If critical information is missing, `scripts/foamagent_intake.py` must return clarify before `src/main.py` is allowed to run.
For Newtonian fluids, `parameters: none required` is invalid; provide at least `nu` and `rho` with source labels.
If Re is above the laminar threshold and turbulence treatment is not confirmed, intake must return clarify.
For rheology/model-fluid benchmarks, if `constitutive_model` is explicit and all required numeric parameters are provided with source labels, material-card lookup is not required. Material cards are only for translating business material names into candidate behavior/model/typical parameters.
Use numeric assignments such as `etaS=0.01`, `etaP=0.99`, `lambda=1.0`; aliases `ηs`, `ηp`, and `λ` are accepted.
